## Full description

Kiebitz looks over your shoulder.



A Kiebitz is a bird, and in German, the onlooker who watches your game over your shoulder. That is what this app does: it pulls your games from chess.com and Lichess, runs a native Stockfish across your whole history, and tells you where you actually play badly. Not "well played", but: which phase, which opening, which time control is where it falls apart.



Your chess data stays on your own devices. Kiebitz has no account or chess-data server; analysis runs locally.



IMPORT, ANALYZE, UNDERSTAND

• Enter your username, that's it. Kiebitz pulls your whole history, duplicate-safe. Over-the-board games come in as PGN.

• A native Stockfish works through the queue in the background: eval per move, inaccuracy, mistake, blunder, accuracy.

• On the phone, analysis runs with leaner settings. Large runs across your whole history are better done on the desktop. The results reach your phone through sync.



INSIGHTS: WHERE YOU ACTUALLY PLAY BADLY

Four in-depth pages across your whole history instead of one number per game.

• Accuracy and error rate over months, not over one game

• Openings with results, deviation and hit rate, split by white and black

• Errors by game phase: opening, middlegame, endgame

• Patterns: time trouble, long sessions, revenge games, opponent strength, weekday and time of day



AN OPENING BOOK THAT ACTUALLY STICKS

• Your repertoire is a position tree trained with FSRS spaced repetition, using the same approach good flashcard apps use

• Every move has its own interval; due reviews are on the dashboard

• Add moves from your own games straight into the book

• Coverage per opening, split by white and black



TACTICS MADE FROM YOUR OWN MISTAKES

• Kiebitz builds problems from the positions where you missed the winning move yourself

• Alongside the offline Lichess puzzle database

• Puzzle Elo and hit rate per theme

• Curated endgame drills against the engine: promotion, rook endings, forced mates, optionally with Syzygy tablebases

• All playable offline, no connection needed



A STUDY PLAN THAT COMES OUT OF YOUR DATA

• The planner reads your weaknesses from the analyses and suggests what to do today: reviews, one theme, one endgame

• Recommendations with reasons taken from your own games

• A daily checklist and a weekly plan you can rearrange yourself

• Reminders run locally on the device



ALSO IN THE APP

• Dashboard: rating history, recent games, due reviews, analysis queue

• Game database: filters, tags, notes per game and per move, position search, PGN import and export

• Live analysis on the board with engine lines, move quality and arrows, optionally with the chessdb.cn opening book

• Seven interface languages, one carefully made dark theme



LOCAL-FIRST, NOT JUST AS A SLOGAN

• No account: no sign-up, no email, no login

• No cloud: games, analyses and progress live in a database on your device

• No automatic crash reports

• Kiebitz connects to chess.com or Lichess for imports, to Lichess for the puzzle download, and, if enabled, to chessdb.cn for the opening book

• Kiebitz Free includes advertising.



DEVICE SYNC WITH NOTHING IN BETWEEN

• Kiebitz is also available as a desktop app for Windows. Phone and desktop sync encrypted, directly over your home network or VPN

• Pairing works with a QR code; address, pairing code and certificate fingerprint are inside it

• The camera is used for that QR scan and nothing else

• Without pairing, the phone works entirely on its own



Kiebitz is actively developed and its source code is publicly viewable. The Stockfish chess engine is licensed under GPL-3.0; the license texts and the written source offer are in the app under Settings → About Kiebitz.
